Privacy Policy

Rosebank Gospel Hall App for Android. Last updated: November 2026.

Who we are

The Rosebank Gospel Hall App is built and maintained by From Gravel Hill Enterprises for Rosebank Gospel Hall, a Gospel Hall Assembly in Rosebank, Prince Edward Island. Questions about this policy can be sent to info@fromgravelhill.ca.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

  • The posts you save
  • Your settings: theme, text size and notification choice
  • A copy of recently loaded posts and pages, so they can be read offline

This information never leaves your phone. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes it.

Connections the app makes

  • rosebankgospelhall.ca and its image delivery service: the app loads posts, pages, pictures and audio from the assembly's website. Like any website, it may keep ordinary server records such as your IP address and the time of the request.
  • YouTube, Vimeo and Rumble: when a post contains a video, the video thumbnail and the player come from that service. Their own privacy policies apply to what they collect when you view their content.
  • GitHub: only if you choose to open the source code or license pages.

All connections use HTTPS.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and off until you turn them on. When on, the app occasionally asks the website whether a new post exists and shows a local notification. No personal information is sent, and no push-messaging service is used.

Permissions

  • Internet access: to load content
  • Notifications: only if you turn on new-post alerts
  • Foreground service (media playback): so audio messages keep playing with the screen off

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
