Rosebank Gospel Hall App

A free Android app for Rosebank Gospel Hall, a Gospel Hall Assembly in Rosebank, Prince Edward Island. Read, watch and listen to messages from rosebankgospelhall.ca in one easy place.

Download for Android Source code

What it does

  • Browse every post with filters, search and sorting
  • Play audio messages in the background, with lock-screen controls
  • Watch videos full screen inside the app
  • Save posts to read again, even offline
  • Read What We Believe, Free Food Hamper and Resources
  • Light or dark theme and adjustable text size
  • Optional alerts when a new post is published

How to install

  1. On your Android phone, open the Download for Android link above.
  2. Under Assets on that page, tap the file ending in .apk.
  3. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.
  4. Updates install over the top and keep your saved posts.

The app is not on Google Play. It needs Android 8.0 or newer.

Check your download
